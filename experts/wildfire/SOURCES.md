# Wildfire Expert: Sources

Sources for ingestion and ongoing monitoring. Organized by type and priority.

## Primary Sources (check weekly)
Last checked: never

### Fire Detection & Monitoring
- **NASA FIRMS**: firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov (active fire data, algorithm updates, new satellite integrations)
- **GOES Fire/Hot Spot product updates**: star.nesdis.noaa.gov (FHS algorithm changes, new products)
- **ALERTWildfire camera network**: alertwildfire.org (coverage expansion, AI detection improvements)

### Fire Modeling & Prediction
- **USFS Fire Modeling Institute**: fs.usda.gov/rmrs/groups/fire-modeling-institute (FARSITE/FlamMap updates, new tools)
- **WRF-SFIRE development**: github.com/openwfm/wrf-fire (code updates, publications)
- **WIFIRE Lab (UCSD)**: wifire.ucsd.edu (ML fire spread, real-time fire modeling)

### AI/ML Fire Research
- **arXiv**: arxiv.org (search: "wildfire" + "machine learning", "fire spread" + "deep learning", "fire detection" + "satellite")
- **IJWF (International Journal of Wildland Fire)**: publish.csiro.au/wf (primary fire science journal)

### Smoke & Air Quality
- **HRRR-Smoke**: rapidrefresh.noaa.gov (model updates, new products)
- **AirFire / BlueSky**: airfire.org (framework updates, operational changes)
- **EPA AirNow Fire & Smoke Map**: airnow.gov (new features, data integration changes)

## Secondary Sources (check monthly)
Last checked: never

### Operational Fire Management
- **NIFC (National Interagency Fire Center)**: nifc.gov (annual statistics, situation reports, policy changes)
- **CAL FIRE**: fire.ca.gov (California fire statistics, prevention programs)
- **InciWeb**: inciweb.wildfire.gov (incident reporting system updates)
- **Canadian Wildland Fire Information System**: cwfis.cfs.nrcan.gc.ca (FWI products, Canadian fire season summaries)

### Fire Weather & Danger Rating
- **SPC Fire Weather Outlook**: spc.noaa.gov (product updates, verification reports)
- **NFDRS updates**: nwcg.gov (system changes, fuel model updates)
- **Copernicus EFFIS**: effis.jrc.ec.europa.eu (European fire danger, global FWI reanalysis)

### Remote Sensing & Satellite Data
- **MTBS (Monitoring Trends in Burn Severity)**: mtbs.gov (new fire perimeter releases, typically annual)
- **Landsat/Sentinel for burn severity**: usgs.gov (new burn area products)
- **VIIRS/MODIS calibration updates**: ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov (collection updates)

### Research Groups & Institutions
- **USFS Pacific Wildland Fire Sciences Lab**: fs.usda.gov/pnw (fire and smoke research, AirFire)
- **NCAR Fire Weather Research**: ncar.ucar.edu (coupled fire-atmosphere modeling)
- **University of Melbourne Bushfire Group**: bushfire.unimelb.edu.au (Phoenix model, Australian fire science)
- **CIRES / NOAA GSL**: cires.colorado.edu (HRRR-Smoke development)

### Conference Proceedings
- **International Association of Wildland Fire (IAWF) conferences** -- Fire behavior, management, and policy
- **AGU Fall Meeting** (December) -- Fire-climate sessions, remote sensing fire detection
- **AMS Annual Meeting** (January) -- Smoke modeling, fire weather sessions
- **Wildland Fire Canada** -- Canadian fire science and operations

### Key Journals
- International Journal of Wildland Fire (primary fire science journal)
- Fire (MDPI open-access fire research journal)
- Remote Sensing of Environment (satellite fire detection methods)
- Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics (smoke transport, air quality)
- Environmental Research Letters (fire-climate interactions)
- Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres (fire emissions, plume dynamics)

## Ingestion Notes

- FIRMS algorithm updates directly impact this expert's claims about detection accuracy and latency.
- HRRR-Smoke is evolving rapidly; operational changes affect smoke forecast guidance.
- AI fire spread papers are appearing with increasing frequency; new claims about outperforming FARSITE should be evaluated against the known limitations in reference/spread-models.md.
- Fire season statistics (NIFC) update annually; always verify when citing recent-year numbers.
- FWI System is stable but NFDRS undergoes periodic updates (fuel model revisions, algorithm changes) that should be tracked.
- During Northern Hemisphere fire season (May-October), active fire information is operationally relevant and should be checked if users ask about current conditions.
