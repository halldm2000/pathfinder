# Wildfire Expert: News

## 2026-03-30
- **Expert v1 deployed** -- Wildfire expert built with 4 reference docs covering fire detection (FIRMS/VIIRS/GOES), fire spread models (FARSITE/FlamMap/WRF-SFIRE/Phoenix/Rothermel), smoke and air quality (HRRR-Smoke/BlueSky/PM2.5/AQI), and fire weather (FWI/NFDRS/Red Flag). Covers the full chain from fire weather conditions through detection, spread prediction, smoke transport, and air quality impact. Designed for chain scenarios with earth2 (weather forecast -> fire risk -> smoke/air quality).
