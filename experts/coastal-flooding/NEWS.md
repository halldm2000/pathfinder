# Coastal Flooding Expert: News

## 2026-03-30
- **Expert v1 deployed** -- Coastal flooding expert built with 4 reference docs covering storm surge models (SLOSH, ADCIRC, SCHISM), sea level rise (IPCC AR6 scenarios, regional projections, observation networks), compound flooding (surge + rain + river interaction, case studies), and coastal data (DEMs, bathymetry, FEMA flood zones, datums). Designed to chain with hurricane expert (TC forcing -> inundation assessment) and ocean expert (sea level, tides). Covers the full coastal flood chain from atmospheric forcing through inundation to risk assessment.
