# Ocean & ENSO Expert: Sources

Sources for ingestion and ongoing monitoring. Organized by type and priority.

## Primary Sources (check weekly)
Last checked: never

### ENSO Monitoring
- **NOAA CPC ENSO page**: cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ (monthly ENSO diagnostic discussion, ONI values)
- **IRI ENSO forecast**: iri.columbia.edu/our-expertise/climate/forecasts/enso/current/ (multi-model ENSO plume, updated monthly)
- **Australian BoM ENSO Outlook**: bom.gov.au/climate/enso/ (independent ENSO assessment)

### Sea Ice
- **NSIDC Arctic Sea Ice News**: nsidc.org/arcticseaicenews/ (monthly updates, extent analysis)
- **NSIDC Sea Ice Index**: nsidc.org/data/seaice_index/ (near-real-time extent and concentration)

### AI Ocean/Climate Models
- **arXiv ocean/climate AI**: arxiv.org (search: "AI ocean model", "coupled climate emulator", "ENSO prediction machine learning", "DLESyM", "SamudrACE")
- **NVIDIA Earth-2 blog**: blogs.nvidia.com/blog/tag/earth-2/ (DLESyM updates, ocean-related releases)
- **Allen AI climate blog**: allenai.org/blog (ACE2, SamudrACE updates)

### Ocean Observations
- **Argo status**: argo.ucsd.edu (fleet status, new float deployments, coverage maps)
- **Copernicus Marine Service**: marine.copernicus.eu (GLORYS12 updates, new products)

## Secondary Sources (check monthly)
Last checked: never

### Ocean Science Research
- **Nature, Science, Nature Communications, Nature Climate Change** (high-impact ocean/climate papers)
- **Journal of Physical Oceanography (JPO)**: journals.ametsoc.org/jpo
- **Journal of Climate**: journals.ametsoc.org/jcli
- **Geophysical Research Letters (GRL)**: agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/grl
- **Journal of Geophysical Research: Oceans (JGR-Oceans)**: agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/jgrc

### AMOC Monitoring
- **RAPID/MOCHA array**: rapid.ac.uk/rapidmoc/ (26.5N overturning time series)
- **OSNAP**: o-snap.org (subpolar overturning observations)

### Ocean Modeling
- **MOM6 GitHub**: github.com/NOAA-GFDL/MOM6 (releases, issues)
- **NEMO releases**: forge.nemo-ocean.eu (version updates, physics changes)
- **HYCOM/GOFS**: hycom.org (operational ocean model updates)

### Satellite Missions
- **Sentinel-6/Jason-CS**: sentinel.esa.int/web/sentinel/missions/sentinel-6 (altimetry continuity)
- **SWOT**: swot.jpl.nasa.gov (science data releases, calibration updates)
- **AMSR2/SSMIS**: nsidc.org (passive microwave sea ice data)

### Conferences (major ocean/climate venues)
- **AGU Fall Meeting** (December) -- Ocean Sciences sessions
- **Ocean Sciences Meeting** (biennial, February) -- primary ocean science conference
- **EGU General Assembly** (April) -- European geoscience including ocean
- **AMS Annual Meeting** (January) -- ocean-atmosphere interaction sessions
- **CLIVAR panels**: clivar.org (ENSO, ocean observation, and modeling community)

## Ingestion Notes

- ENSO state is the most time-sensitive: CPC updates monthly, and teleconnection guidance depends on current phase.
- Sea ice extent is highly seasonal: check weekly during melt season (Jun-Sep Arctic, Jan-Mar Antarctic).
- AI ocean models are a nascent field -- new papers may represent significant capability leaps.
- Ocean reanalysis products (GLORYS12, ORAS5) update periodically; major version changes affect training data for AI models.
- AMOC observations accumulate slowly -- RAPID publishes annual updates, not real-time.
