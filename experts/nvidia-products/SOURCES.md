- **GTC** (March): NVIDIA flagship -- major product announcements, roadmap updates
- **CES** (January): consumer and enterprise AI announcements
- **Computex** (May-June): hardware partnerships, OEM announcements
- **SC (Supercomputing)** (November): HPC, inference, networking announcements
- **Hot Chips** (August): GPU architecture deep-dives

## Ingestion Notes

- GTC is the highest-signal event -- plan freshness checks 1 week before and after.
- NIM catalog changes are highest frequency -- new NIMs ship monthly.
- GPU availability claims need verification -- "announced" != "shipping" != "widely available in cloud."
- NVIDIA AI Enterprise version changes affect licensing and deployment guidance.
- Competitive landscape shifts (AMD MI350, Intel Falcon Shores) affect product recommendations.
- TensorRT-LLM and Triton releases directly impact NIM capabilities.
